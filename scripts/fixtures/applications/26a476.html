Oct 14 2026 Application (26A476) for stay presented to The Chief Justice and by him referred to the Court is granted. The October 7, 2026 judgment of the United States Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit, case No. 26–2230, is stayed pending the filing and disposition of a petition for a writ of certiorari, if such a writ is timely sought. Should certiorari be denied, this stay shall terminate automatically. In the event that certiorari is granted, the stay shall terminate upon the sending down of the judgment of this Court. Justice Sotomayor and Justice Jackson would deny the application. Opinion per curiam. (Detached Opinion)
